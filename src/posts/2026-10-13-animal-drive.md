We're teaming up with **ACTion Programs for Animals (APA)** for an animal drive at our October 24th show.

**What we're collecting:** canned pet food, dry pet food, pet toys and blankets.

Every item donated earns one raffle ticket — unlimited entries. **Three lucky winners** will each receive a $50 voucher to spend at the show. All donated items go directly to APA to help animals in need.
