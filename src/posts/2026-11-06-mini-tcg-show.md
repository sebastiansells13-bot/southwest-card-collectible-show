The Southwest Card & Collectible Show is back with our **first-ever Mini TCG Show** on **Saturday, November 28th** at the Mesilla Community Center, 11 AM to 5 PM! 🎴⚡

Come buy, sell, trade and collect with 26 tables filled with Pokémon, One Piece, Dragon Ball, Magic, Lorcana, Naruto and everything else TCG.

📅 Saturday, November 28 · 📍 Mesilla Community Center · 🎟️ $2 entry, kids 12 & under free · 🎁 Free raffles all day (over $500 in items)

👀 Be on the lookout: we'll reveal our giveaways and the official vendor map closer to show day. This is our first event of this kind, so make sure you stop by! And sports card fans — we didn't forget about you. 👀
