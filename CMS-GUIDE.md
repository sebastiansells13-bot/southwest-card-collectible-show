# Editing your website

You can update the Southwest Card & Collectible Show website yourself — no code needed.

## 1. Log in

1. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org/edit/sebastiansells13-bot/southwest-card-collectible-show**
2. Sign in with your GitHub account (your developer will add you to the project first)

## 2. What you can edit

- **Shows & Trade Days** — add each new show or trade day: name, date, hours, venue,
  address, flyer and a few highlight bullets. The next upcoming event is featured on the
  home page automatically, and past events move to "Past events" on their own.
- **News** — announcements and recaps (basically your Instagram posts). Each one has a
  title, short summary, date, image and text.
- **Home Page** — the intro paragraph, the three perks, the table-by-table breakdown and
  the "Giving back" section.
- **Table Booking (Pick-a-Table)** — this is where you open and close vendor sign-ups.
  - **Taking Vendor Requests** is the on/off switch. Turn it ON when you're ready to
    take vendors for the next show: the floor map and request form appear on the
    Vendors page. Turn it OFF and they disappear; vendors see your "Message While
    Closed" instead (and the "Banner While Closed", e.g. "Tables are SOLD OUT").
  - When you confirm a vendor, add their table under **Reserved Tables** (number,
    status "Taken", vendor name) and it greys out on the map. Use "Pending" while
    you're waiting on payment.
  - For a new show, update the show name and date and clear Reserved Tables to start
    with a blank map.
- **Vendors Page** — the intro, last show's vendor map image and the FAQ.
- **Sponsors** — add, remove or reorder sponsors, with an optional logo and link.
- **About Page** — your story.
- **Contact & Social Links** — Instagram, Discord, and optional email/phone.

## 3. Spanish

Visitors can switch the whole site to Spanish with the **EN / ES** button at the top.
Most fields have a matching **(Spanish)** field right below the English one. Fill it in
and Spanish visitors see your translation; leave it blank and they see the English
text. If you change the English, update the Spanish too.

## 4. Making a change

1. Click the section you want (e.g. "Shows & Trade Days")
2. Click an item to edit it, or **Add** to create a new one
3. Fill in the fields and click **Save**

The site updates itself 1–3 minutes after you save. Refresh the page if you don't see
it yet.

## 5. Tips

- **Announcing a new show?** Add it under Shows & Trade Days, then add a News post with
  the flyer. When tables sell out, turn off "Taking Vendor Requests" and set the closed banner to "Tables are SOLD OUT".
- **Images**: use the upload button. Your Instagram flyers work great as-is. Keep files
  under ~1MB — the site compresses them further when it publishes.

## 6. Getting help

If something looks wrong after saving, contact your developer rather than editing files
directly on GitHub.
